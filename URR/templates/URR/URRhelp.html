{% translate "Playing the Game" %}

{% translate "Select a hex, player, or state to inspect it. Your actions stay in a local draft: Undo and Reset Turn change the draft, End Turn saves it. Leaving the page before End Turn discards the draft." %}

{% translate "Waterworks on another player's land become requests that the landowner accepts or declines on their turn." %}

{% translate "History lists every move with its payments. Replay steps through earlier positions without changing the game." %}

{% translate "When can I resign?" %}

{% translate "Only on your own turn during Settlement. A resigned or kicked-out player keeps their land and money; their remaining turns are passed automatically." %}
