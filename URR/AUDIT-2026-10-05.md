# URR (UR: 1830 BC) — Rule Implementation Audit

Date: 2026-10-05
Rulebook: `URR/Ur1830Rules.md` (All-Aboard / Splotter, 2nd edition)
Code under audit: `URR/vueURR/src/**` (client rules engine), `URR/views.py`, `URR/common.py`
Status: **report only — no code changed**

## Method

- Section-by-section read of the rulebook against the engine.
- Targeted scripted replays of every worked example in the rulebook.
- 25-game random-legal-move fuzzer over complete games (division → game over).
- 60 synthetic rainy-season routing games spanning all five eras.
- `node rulesCheck.mjs` run unmodified — passes.
- Printed board (`URR/static/URR/images/ur-map.png`) inspected for the nation
  homelands, city sites and confluences.

Because the client owns the rules in this repo (see `FOUNDATION.md`), "implemented"
here means *enforced in `applyAction` and reachable through the normal controls* —
not enforced server-side.

## Verdict

The engine is in good shape. Every worked example in the rulebook replays exactly,
and the rainy season is watertight under fuzzing. One genuine rule is missing
(reservoirs on a river confluence). Two economy/interpretation issues and one
unresolved rules conflict remain. Four balance-critical constants cannot be
verified from anything in this repository.

## Gaps

### 1. Reservoirs on a river confluence — rule not implemented · high

§5.3.1.1: *"If the river area has a confluence (where two rivers meet), the reservoir
must be placed downstream of the confluence."*

Accepted today on `E7` (rivers 1 and 3 meet) and `H4` (rivers 1 and 2 meet) — the
only two confluences on the board. There is no UI guard either, so the button is
live. `H4` matters strategically: it intercepts the combined flow of all three
rivers at once.

Fix shape: flag river areas fed by more than one upstream (derivable from
`board.riverDownstream`, which already drives confluence waiting in `URRwater.js`),
then extend the reservoir/river check at `URRgame.js:315`.

### 2. Non-leading offers are not reserved · medium

`availableMoney` (`URRrules.js:30-38`) reserves only the **highest** bid per nation.
A player with 115 SPL on the card behind someone else's 120 is reported as having
all 600 SPL free, contradicting *"the amount offered and the player's ownership
marker are placed below the nation's card"* and the worked example's *"player 2
regains use of their 115 SPL"*.

Not exploitable. I tried: a trailing bidder must raise in order to win (the raise is
funds-checked), and single-bid auto-awards keep the reservation intact, so no
reachable double-spend exists. `NationMarket.moneyForBid` inherits the error and
amplifies it in the displayed figure, and the auto-withdraw test in
`getAutomaticAction` becomes over-optimistic.

Fix shape: sum every bid a player holds across all nations.

### 3. Player-owned Eridu cannot dig from its own homeland · medium, needs a ruling

`isNationLandClosed` closes all nation land to crews while a nation is player-owned,
so `I7`, `I8` and `I9` are unusable dig origins for the player holding Eridu. A
state-owned Eridu digs out of them without trouble. §5.2's blanket rule and §9.5's
*"acts as a 2-digging crew in all respects"* genuinely conflict, and the literal
reading makes the card nearly inert for a player.

Unresolved. Needs the official Splotter FAQ or a ruling at the table. Note the
precedent cuts both ways: §9.2 explicitly grants Barahshum a homeland dig, §9.5
does not mention one for Eridu.

If allowed: exempt the digging crew's own nation in `isNationLandClosed`, the way
`exchangeBarahshum` already does.

### 4. Stale auction award if the leading bidder withdraws · low, unreachable

`awardNation(game, nation, auction.highPlayer, auction.amount)` would hand the
nation to a player who just withdrew, at the pre-withdrawal amount. Unreachable
through legal play, because `setAuctionTurn` never asks the leading bidder while
another bidder remains, so it only bites a hand-edited or legacy save. Related:
`awardNation` performs no funds assertion.

### 5. Four unverifiable, balance-changing constants · medium risk

None of these have a source anywhere in this repository:

- `LAND_PRICE_TRACK` and `LAND_INTERVENTION_PRICE = 46` (`URRreference.js:109-110`) —
  transcribed from a BGG chart PDF. The rulebook pins only the colonization prices,
  the §4.4 example and the §4.7 example; all three match, the remainder of the bar
  is unconfirmed.
- `board.waterworkLimits` (`URRboard.js:79`) — per-state token counts per capacity,
  from a "printed markers sheet".
- `board.stateOrder` (`URRboard.js:77`) — Elam, Sumer, Akkad, Babylon, Persia, Urartu
  as printed numbers 1–6. Decides every development-order tie.
- `PRINTED_MARKER_LIMIT = 30` — from a BGG README, not the rulebook, which only
  says "ownership markers for six players".

All four decide real outcomes. One pass against the physical components would close
this out.

### 6. Der and Calah homelands straddle two states · cosmetic

Der is hills Urartu plus hills Akkad; Calah is hills Urartu plus forest Babylon.
Inspecting the printed map, those dashed nation outlines do appear to cross, so this
may well be correct. Gameplay impact is nil, because nation land is state-agnostic
in the engine — First Akkadians is the only exception and is correctly three Akkad
savannahs. The only artifact is the "find on the board" button, which shows the
first homeland area's state.

### 7. Nits

- **Forced maintenance is the worst interaction in the game.** The monarch must
  submit the exact multi-batch combination that covers the shortfall in a single
  action. There is no "sell everything eligible" or "declare revolution" button,
  only a preview error string. Both fuzzers deadlocked on this repeatedly before
  being taught the batch construction. Correct, but hostile.
- `resolveMaintenance` reports *"The last player-owned land in a state cannot be
  sold"* for batches that actually failed on the terrain or batch rules, because
  its per-area pre-check runs before `sellLand`'s own checks.
- Debug cheats are live in real games for `admin` and `BotKickStarter`
  (`URRreference.js:8`, `URRdebug.js:7`).
- `common.py:110` hard-forces `isTrainingGame = True`, so no real multiplayer URR
  game can currently be created. Presumably intentional for the playtest build —
  worth confirming.

## Test coverage gaps in `rulesCheck.mjs`

No assertions for the §4.4 and §4.7 price examples, the confluence rule, multi-offer
cash reservation, Eridu's homeland digging, or the era 3/4/M waterwork-supply
interaction. The file is a good pattern to extend whenever any of the above is fixed.

## Suggested order of work, if any of it is taken up

1. Confluence rule — the only truly unimplemented rule; smallest and safest change.
2. Offer reservation plus the matching `NationMarket` money display.
3. Eridu ruling, then the one-line `isNationLandExempt` change.
4. A "sell all eligible / declare revolution" button in the maintenance panel.
5. `rulesCheck.mjs` assertions for items 1–3 and the two price examples.
