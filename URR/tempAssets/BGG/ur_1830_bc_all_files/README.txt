You'll also need:

- 30 ownership (player) markers in 6 colours (ideally different ones than Nations' (Sumer, Persia, Babylon, etc.)). I use wooden 8mm cubes. Originally they are double-sided - you should marke one of the sides (I use small circular stickers)

- 24 blue discs (10-15mm of diameter)

- Something to mark canals (ideally blue wooden sticks)

- Money (poker chips)

---

- Diggers and Independent nations cards are of mini american size (41mm x 63mm). There are two versions of diggers to choose from.

- Markers represent waterworks. Two files are provided, one for cutting rectangular markerse and the other (_no_outline) for cutting circular ones.
