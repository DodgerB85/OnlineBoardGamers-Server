You'll need also:

- game board - I use one posted on BGG forum: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1802736/half-assed-pnp-map

- 20-30 player markers in 6 colours (ideally different ones than Nations' (Sumer, Persia, Babylon, etc.)). I use wooden cubes 8mm x 8mm x 8mm. Either you mark them on one side or you get yet another colour to indicate sold regions (normally the markers are two-sided). 20 markers per colour should be enough, originally it was 30 though.

- 24 blue discs (10-15mm of diameter)

- something to mark canals (ideally blue wooden sticks)

- money (poker chips)

---

Diggers and Independent nations cards are of mini american size (41mm x 63mm). There are two versions of diggers to choose from.
