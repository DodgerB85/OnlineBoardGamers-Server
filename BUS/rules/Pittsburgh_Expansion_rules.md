# Bus: Pittsburgh Expansion — Rules

> Converted from `BUS/static/BUS/Bus_Pitts_Rules.pdf` (2 pages) on 2026-10-24.
> Fan-made map by Dean Brandt; it is not part of the base game.
> Diagrams are not included; consult the PDF for the map markings.

## Setup

Place one Passenger on each of the four building locations marked on the map.

## One-Way Streets

Some streets display directional arrows. During the Vrrooomm! action, Passengers can only be transported along those streets in the direction indicated by the arrows.

- Line Expansion may be performed in either direction, regardless of arrows.

## Bridges

There are five white Bridge line markers and nine designated Bridge spaces on the game map. A Bridge may be placed on any unoccupied Bridge space at one of the ends of a player's line by using one of their Bus Line marker placements granted by the Line Expansion action.

- Placing a Bridge does not consume the entire action unless the Player only has one (1) Line Expansion.
- Bridges may not be placed during the first line marker placements (initial setup).
- A bridge may never be placed on a crossing that is not one of the ends of the player's line.

### Expanding After a Bridge

- If a Bridge exists at the end of your bus line, then during your next Line Expansion action, you may choose either to:
  - Expand your line from the far end of the Bridge, or
  - Expand from the last line marker of your line before the Bridge.
- Whichever option you choose becomes the new end of your line.

### Forced Bridge Continuation

- A Bridge is not considered the end of a line when determining legal positions for placing a line marker unless:
  - The only empty street from the end of your bus line is a Bridge space, and
  - At least one Bridge Bus Line marker is available.
- If both conditions are met, you must expand your line across that Bridge. If no Bridge Bus Line marker is available, standard Line Expansion rules apply.

### Using Bridges

- Once placed, a Bridge can be used by any player during the Vrrooomm! action to pick up and deliver passengers, provided the Bridge is connected to their line.
  - Bridges may be entered or exited even from a junction.

## Splotter Designers

### Airport

- The Airport is the large circular junction in Moon Township that also functions as a Train Station.
- When resolving the Passengers action, a player may bring into play one Splotter Designer (Jeroen or Joris) at the Airport instead of bringing two regular passengers into play.
  - Only one Designer may arrive at the Airport per round.
- If a player has additional passengers to place after placing a Designer, those passengers may be placed at either of the two stations, as per the standard rules.

### Transporting Designers

- Designers (and any accompanying passengers) can be transported during the same round in which they are placed, using the Vrrooomm! action.
- Designers follow the normal rules for transporting passengers and may be delivered to houses, offices, or pubs while visiting Pittsburgh.

### Attending Splotter Con

- Jeroen can be transported to the Splotter Con space if the clock is set to Pubs (his meeple is black to align with the Pub tokens).
- Joris may be transported to the Splotter Con space if the clock is set to Offices (his meeple is dark grey to align with the Office tokens).
- Delivering a Designer to Splotter Con:
  - Scores 2 points if delivered in the same round they were placed at the Airport (they don't want to be late!)
  - Scores 1 point if delivered in a later round
- Each Designer may attend Splotter Con only once per game. Both Designers may attend Splotter Con simultaneously.

### Returning to the Netherlands

- Only after attending Splotter Con, a Designer can be transported back to the Airport during a Vrrooomm! action, following the normal rules for transporting passengers.
  - Jeroen will only visit the airport when transporting passengers to pubs
  - Joris will only visit the airport when transporting passengers to offices
- Returning a Designer to the Airport scores 1 point. Once returned, that Designer is removed from the game and may not be transported again.
