Implemented the &c production decisions and the electricity/management audit fixes in vueRNB source. No dist build was run or touched.

Production setup
- Separate main phase 17 (ordered as 2.5), after the production conflict/turn order and before production.
- In turn order, each player with a choice announces transported managers and transporter fuel.
- Choices are stored in STACK_PRODUCTION_SETUP; pre-set production saves them as a separate setup move and replay applies them before production.
- Players with nothing to choose are skipped; production capacities are recalculated after the choices.

Electricity
- Power plant: 3 stone, 1 board/trunk per turn, board first, one fuel per plant, no effect on secondary producers or reproduction.
- Tile fuel is consumed before the mines pull; a transporter can fuel a plant during the setup phase.
- The power network is bucket-level: river banks only connect through a bridge, and a city is one power node (BGG 808497).
- Free tile fuel and carried fuel only use the plant's own side of a river; a city sawmill keeps its trunks.
- Power-line build highlighting mirrors roads on river edges: each bank is a separate lane, so a line can be laid on a bank without a bridge; the bucket network still requires a bridge for the power to cross.
- Power-line build/verify and setup replay now check bridges, transporter ownership and extra mine output shape.
- Removed the unused pendingTransporterFuel path.

Management
- MBA (Coast) is a shore/river building, not an open-sea building.
- Managers: one effect per tile, direct managers always active, carried managers decided by their transporter owner, all secondary producers on the tile doubled (both city buildings), never consumed.
- Full &c city production order is applied to auto-secondary production: sawmill, power plant, paper mill, coal burner, mint, stock, raft factory, wagon factory, rowboat factory, truck factory, steamer factory, airport, MBA, bomb factory, atelier; research last. Replays of old city games may recompute a different auto-production order than the original save.
- City bomb destroys both unstrengthened buildings; strengthened buildings survive.
- City order is applied to auto-secondary production and the sawmill/plant trunk conflict. A transporter can fuel a plant with its own wood, but cannot reserve a tile board that the plant auto-consumed.
- Production-setup panel strings added for de, es, fr, it and zh-hans.

Save/replay
- productionDecisions, working power plants and the setup stack action round-trip; powered buckets are recomputed from the working plants on load.
- Replay resets production capacities at production start and applies the setup action before the player's production actions.
- Old saves that relied on hex-level power across an unbridged river now follow the bucket rule: the far bank is not powered.

Verification
- RNB frontend tests: vitest + node suites pass. A throwaway Vite build to a temp directory succeeded; the temp output was deleted and RNB/static/RNB/RNBvuedist was not touched.
- Pre-existing ESLint errors remain in RNBmap.js, RNBmodel.js, RNBstack.js and MapArea.vue; the changed logic adds none.

Timing
