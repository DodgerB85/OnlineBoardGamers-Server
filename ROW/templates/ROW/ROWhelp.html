{% translate "Overview" %}

{% blocktranslate %}Ranchers of the Old West is an online board game hosted by Online Board Gamers. Each player is a cattleman driving a herd north to Kansas City while building up their deck, hiring workers, laying track and placing buildings. When Kansas City is reached the herd is sold and the player rides back to the start to do it again. The player with the most victory points when the game ends wins.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "A turn in three phases" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} 1. Move. Move your cattleman 1 or more steps forward along the trail. You cannot move backwards. Spaces with no tile are not counted as steps. If you move onto or over a location showing a hand icon you pay a fee (green/black hands differ by player count); fees on hazards and teepees go to the bank, fees on an opponent's building go to that player. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} 2. Take an action. Depending on where you stopped you may use the local action printed on the tile, or take a single unlocked auxiliary action from your player board, or (if you stopped on an opponent's building, a hazard or a teepee) only an auxiliary action. Actions separated by a divider can each be used once; a split action means you choose one side. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} 3. Draw up. Draw cards until your hand matches your hand limit. Do not reshuffle the discard pile until you actually need to draw and the draw stack is empty. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Kansas City" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Stopping at Kansas City resolves five steps in order: choose the tiles in foresight spots 1, 2 and 3; then sell your herd (add up the breeding value of each different cattle type in hand, plus any certificates you choose to spend) and copy your hand to your discard pile; then deliver to a city whose value your herd covers. Removing a disc unlocks the upgrade printed on your player board. The engine then travels back to the start. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Common actions" %}

  • {% translate "Hire a worker from the job market (the whole row under the job market token is locked)." %}
  • {% translate "Buy cattle from the cattle market, single or pair, using cowboys for better cards." %}
  • {% translate "Place or upgrade a private building; the craftsmen in your row must cover the cost." %}
  • {% translate "Move your engine forward (up to the number of engineers you have) and upgrade a station you stop on." %}
  • {% translate "Trade with the tribes and remove hazards." %}
  • {% translate "Take objective cards and play them for their bonus; completed objectives score points, incomplete ones cost points." %}

{% translate "Using the online board" %}

  • {% translate "Click a highlighted trail spot to move your cattleman. Cyan spots are more than one step away." %}
  • {% translate "When several actions are possible the buttons appear in the action bar under the board. Pick one, then click the card, board space or track space it needs." %}
  • {% translate "Hover a building or heartland tile on the board to see its actions." %}
  • {% translate "Use Undo to step back one action, or Reset Whole Turn to go back to the start of your turn." %}
  • {% translate "The History button lists recent actions, Chat talks to the table, Notes are private to you, and the magnifier buttons zoom the board." %}

{% translate "Scoring at the end" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Final points come from cash ($1 per $5), cattle cards, objective cards, station masters, workers in the last two slots of each row, hazards, buildings on the board, cities you delivered to, upgraded stations, the job-market token and any extra-step bonus. Ties are shared. {% endblocktranslate %}
