Devil's Egg Tournament Rules

This year, we will be transitioning to web-based reporting but will use the stamp system as backup. This means all of your games must be added within our scheduling system and recorded as Completed Games to count.

As part of your Con Swag Bag, you will receive a Player Aid. Find the Devil's Egg tracking sheet inside.

  • You only score points for games that you win!
    • We encourage everyone to keep track of all qualified wins as ya never know.
  • The six games included in the 2026 tournament are: Antiquity, Bus, Food Chain Magnate (original or Ketchup), Horseless Carriage, Indonesia, and Roads & Boats.
  • Up to three wins per title can be counted during preliminary rounds.
    • After each game you win, record the tournament points earned (using the table below) and the day of your game on your tournament sheet.
    • Points are rewarded to the winner of each game only: 1000 points per win plus additional points per player (see table below). Only the player counts listed for each game will score.
    • At the end of each day, add your daily points total to the Tournament Scoreboard.
  • Cutoff Time: Points online will freeze on Sunday @ 11PM. We will sum all of your points across the three days. If you dispute the scores listed on our website, bring your Player Aid to Dean for review before the end of Sunday night.
  • The 4 players with the most points at the end of Sunday night will meet in a final showdown on Monday at 9:00 AM. The final tournament game this year will be Bus: Pittsburgh.
    • If we have 5 or more tied players, we'll select the 4 players with the most Bus wins during the preliminary rounds. If there is still a tie, the second tie breaker will be highest qualifying Bus score. Final tie breaker will go to player with most Indonesia wins during qualifiers.
    • The players' moves during this final match will be live broadcast on onlineboardgamers.com, and will be available afterward to review.
    • Strategy advice to other players during the final match is prohibited. Non-strategy table talk is permissible. This rule does not apply to preliminary games.
  • 1st place winner will receive the 2026 Devil's Egg Trophy and will have first pick of prizes donated by our generous sponsors. The other three finalists will receive early prize selections.
