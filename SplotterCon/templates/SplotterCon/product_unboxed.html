Description

After nearly a year of deep research, late-night editing, and generous contributions from the Splotter community, we're thrilled to announce Splotter Unboxed: A Complete Compendium of Game Editions Through 2025.

This 140 page book is a passion project — one that dives into every edition of every game Splotter has ever published. It's packed with meticulously compiled component tables, behind-the-scenes stories from Jeroen and Joris, and a treasure trove of amazing photography from Henk Rolleman (Kiek photographer), Larry Rippel & Tamara van Es (former Splotter designer/founder). The incredibly talented Brooke Schooles compiled everything into a beautiful design. This has been a massive team effort, and we can't wait to share it with you.

We're thrilled to announce that our friends at Travel-Games.Co.UK are carrying Splotter Unboxed. This should make shipping much cheaper for those of you in the UK and Europe.
