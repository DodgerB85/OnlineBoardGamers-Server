Donate or Lend Games

We are still growing our Games Library, and thus, welcome your donations and loans.

How it works

Tell us whether you'd like to donate or lend a game (or games) to the Splotter Con library, and we'll coordinate with you before the con.

Games we're looking for — the full Splotter catalogue:

  • Antiquity, Beest, Bus, Cannes, Chameleo Chameleo, Duck Dealer, Food Chain Magnate, FCM: Ketchup, Gossip, The Great Zimbabwe, Greed Incorporated, Horseless Carriage, Indonesia, Kiek, Oraklos, Roads & Boats, Spöl, Tetragons, Ur: 1830 BC, VOC!, Web

Editions: 1st Ed, 2nd Ed, 3rd Ed, Anniversary Ed, VHS Ed, Print & Play, or Unsure.

Condition: New, Very Good, Box Damage but Game VG, Complete & Playable but Fair, or Parts.

Get in touch

  • Which game(s)

  • Condition
