Splotter Con 2026 is dedicated to the memory of Adam Kramer — a dear friend, devoted Splotter collector, and one of the convention's earliest volunteers, whose presence helped shape our community from the very beginning. His spirit remains a part of this gathering, and we honor his memory by dedicating this year's convention to the values he so fully embodied. ~Dean Brandt, Splotter Con Organizer

Splotter Con is a small, community-focused heavy board game convention in Pittsburgh for players who love deep economic and strategy games. It's built for people who want more than a crowded hall: more games played, more meaningful conversation, and a friendlier pace where it's easy to meet others and get the right group to the table.

The weekend centers on a curated library of titles (including Splotter classics and modern heavy favorites), organized play opportunities, and light structure that helps everyone feel included, whether you're bringing a group or arriving solo. Expect tournaments and contests, prizes and swag, and plenty of space to play well into the night.

If you enjoy challenging games and great company, you'll fit right in.

Splotter Con, while having the publisher's blessing and support, is not directly affiliated with Splotter Spellen. Splotter Con is organized independently by Splotter Con LLC, a USA-based business.
