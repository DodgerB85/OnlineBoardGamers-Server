About Splotter Con

First of all, thank you for visiting our site. Hopefully you've found answers to all of your questions. If you haven't, please reach out to us.

Splotter Con rose out of my passion for games designed by Dutch publisher, Splotter Spellen – Indonesia, Bus, and The Great Zimbabwe are all in my Top 6 All Time Boardgames.

While attending Winsome Choose Some Con in Spring 2023, I pitched the idea for a similar convention but celebrating Splotter's boardgames rather than Winsome's to one of the designers, Jeroen, who happened to be in Pittsburgh for the same con. After some further discussion with his partner, Joris, Splotter Con was born. We held our first event at the Green Tree DoubleTree over Labor Day weekend in 2024. It was a resounding success by all of my metrics but if you're interested, I wrote a full con report on boardgamegeek.com.

Fueling me is my desire to bring together other like-minded game players from around the world to celebrate the amazing creativity and cleverness of Jeroen Doumen, Herman Haverkort, Tamara Jannink, and Joris Wiersinga.

At some point, I also became a collector of Splotter's games (which I now own all of, apart from various editions), and thought it would be nice if I could share all of these obscure, harder-to-find titles with other fans.

I set out with three primary intentions: 1) attract a kind, diverse, communal group of players and collectors, 2) encourage folks to have fun teaching and sharing their love for Splotter's games, and 3) host a light, playful competition without the intensity of a full on tournament.

Thanks to my wonderful local gaming community for their incredible support, to all of our generous sponsors, and to Splotter for giving me this opportunity.

I hope to see you in Pittsburgh!

~Dean Brandt, Organizer

Splotter Con 2026 Highlights

Every Splotter +

Splotter Con's game library features multiple copies of every game designed by Splotter, with a variety of editions and languages, plus a selection of games that inspired the designers, a curated collection donated by our sponsors, and a local section. This is your opportunity to try every Splotter!

Global Board Game Community

This is an opportunity to meet, play with, and befriend other Splotter fans, collectors & players from around the world. We expect both competitive play amongst experienced players and casual learning games. We pride ourselves on ensuring a welcoming environment all attendees. Many of us have made lifelong friends with others who share in their love for heavier, strategic games.

Win Amazing Prizes from our Partners!

Multiple Fun Contests

Milestones (most Splotters played during weekend), Spelgid (most Splotters taught), Bus Driver (most different competitors) & Path of the Polder Pioneer (unlock special Splotter Con moments).

Devil's Egg -- Our Flagship Tournament

Play Food Chain Magnate, Indonesia, Horseless Carriage, Bus, Antiquity & Roads & Boats. Score points for your wins. Top four points earners will play in a final match of Bus: Pittsburgh, broadcast live via onlineboardgamers.com, to determine the winner. Winner will receive the coveted Devil's Egg trophy & first overall prize selection. Three other finalists will receive early prize selection.

The Great Zimbabwe Tournament

Condensed, competitive play — 3 rounds max (Saturday / Sunday). Winner will receive a trophy & early prize selection.

D'r af Mini Tourney

Lighthearted, single-day (Sunday) tournament. All four finalists will receive a complementary copy of Worm Up!, the inspirational predecessor to D'r af.

Special Guest: Jeroen Doumen!

At least half of the Splotter design team (Jeroen Doumen) is planning to attend Splotter Con 2026 (barring any curveballs). Come meet, play games with, and get your games signed by him.

Single Location: Downtown Pittsburgh

Our new hotel is conveniently located in Downtown Pittsburgh within walking distance to over 75 restaurants, Point State Park, Acrisure Stadium, PNC Park & PPG Paints Arena. Lots to love about this hotel.

Pittsburgh is known both as "the Steel City" for its dominant role in the history of the U.S. steel industry, and as the "City of Bridges" for its 446 bridges. The city features 30 skyscrapers, two inclined railways, a pre-revolutionary fortification and Point State Park at the confluence of the rivers. (wikipedia) (Photo by Vidar Nordli-Mathisen)

Swag Bag & Merch

All attendees will receive a Splotter Con swag bag along with a couple goodies. Con t-shirts are being designed by Japanese board game artist, Sai Beppu (artist for Web 2e). Attendees will also be the first to hold and play the first copies of Gossip! 2nd edition.
