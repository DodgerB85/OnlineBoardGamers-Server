Prize Distribution & General Rules

Applicable to All Splotter Con Contests & Tournaments

We are continuing our tradition of highly engaging contests and tournaments at this year's Splotter Con. In addition to our four contests and flagship Devil's Egg Tournament, we are also launching a dedicated The Great Zimbabwe Tournament this year. Below are some general rules, guidance, and explanation of prize distribution for all contests and tournaments.

Overview & General Rules

  • As part of your Con Swag Bag, you will receive a Player Aid where you can track your contest & tournament progress.
  • All prizes will be distributed at our Wrap Up event on Monday at 11:00 AM (or once the Devil's Egg Tournament Final is finished).
  • Only one prize per attendee. If you win multiple contests / tournaments, you are permitted to select your highest placement in order to receive an earlier prize.
  • Due the feedback about the Wrap Up event's length and because attendance has grown significantly since our first year, we will be limiting prizes to the top 5 finishers in each contest & the four finalists in each tournament.
  • You must be present to pick up your prize. This is to encourage folks to stick around for the entire con.
  • The prizes will be on display the entire weekend so please determine a handful of items you'd like if you win a prize. Because in order to move things along swiftly, each prize winner will be asked to select a prize immediately upon being called.

Prize Distribution Order

  • Before we distribute prizes from our sponsors, we will select the winner of the Path of the Polder Pioneer contest who will receive a complimentary badge to Splotter Con 2027.
  • Prize selection will proceed in a rotating order, beginning with the 1st-place winners and continuing through the 4th-place winners of each event. The rotation order is as follows: Devil's Egg Tournament → Milestones → Spelgids → The Great Zimbabwe Tournament → Bus Driver
  • After all 1st–4th place winners have selected prizes, 5th-place finishers from the following contests will receive prizes, in this order: Milestones → Spelgids → Bus Driver
