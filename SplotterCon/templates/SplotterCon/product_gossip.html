Description

In GOSSIP!, players whisper cards across backyard fences in this delightfully nosy neighborhood, hoping to be seen with the right residents and avoid getting tangled up with the more questionable ones. Each round, reputations rise and tumble as some are caught Dating or Seen Together, dragged into awkward Public Disputes, and targeted by sneaky Silent Treatments that ripple through the cul-de-sac, crowning The Darling while someone else becomes the neighborhood Outsider.

Play your cards shrewdly to flatter the popular folks, undermine rivals, and avoid becoming the talk of the block, because every rumor you spread might boost your standing. Or send it crashing into the gutter. When that fateful STOP! card finally surfaces and the gossip settles, only one Darling will shine brightest!
