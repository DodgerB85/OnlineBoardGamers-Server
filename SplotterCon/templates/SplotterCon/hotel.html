Why Wyndham Grand?

We chose the Wyndham Grand Pittsburgh Downtown based on thoughtful feedback from prior attendees and our ongoing commitment to improving the Splotter Con experience for our community. The hotel offers the right balance of spacious, well-suited gaming areas and comfortable on-site accommodations, allowing everyone to stay, play, and socialize under one roof. Its downtown location keeps food, coffee, and transit within easy walking distance while minimizing friction during the weekend.
