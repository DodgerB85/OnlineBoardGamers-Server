{% blocktranslate %}Eclipse is played on a hosted table backed by the Eclipse Discord bot. The lobby here handles accounts, invitations and joining; the game itself, its history and its Discord channels live in the bot. The table page is the same game the Discord channels mirror.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Discord is required" %}

{% blocktranslate %}Every player must link their Discord account in their profile and be a member of the OBG Discord server before creating or joining an Eclipse game. When the last seat joins, OBG creates the Discord category, role, table-talk channel, actions channel and faction draft. Players can act from the table page or from Discord; both surfaces share the same prompt ids.{% endblocktranslate %}
