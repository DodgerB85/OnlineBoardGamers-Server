Opening Milestones
This table shows the relative win to loss rates when a particular opening is chosen.
Of course, if an opening is very popular, it will show up as a win and (potentially multiple) losses.
This is for all players. See 2 player data for more head to head results.
The higher the win to loss ratio, approximately the better the opening.
The original MS openings considered are:
  • First Train AND First Hire 3
  • First Hire 3 (but not First Train)
  • First Train (but not First Hire 3)
2 Player stats give a slighly better indication of how openings perform, as they are head to head.
Of course, they can still be skewed if one opening is picked most of the time.
