Reading and reconstruction rules
  • Crops preserve original backgrounds; these are reference assets, not transparent sprites.
  • Meanings were checked against the full annotated PDF, including its errata. Page references are the printed page numbers (also the 1-based PDF page numbers in this file).
  • Objective colours are victory categories (§16, p. 24); every colour in a stacked group counts independently. They do not indicate current ownership.
  • Approach marks are sided. Matching unit-type penalties normally apply only when the defense approach is blocked (p. 16); cavalry obstruction checks the defense side (p. 13), while a declared Guard Attack checks obstruction on the attack side (p. 23).
  • Capacity is a whole-locale unit limit, not approach width or strength. The road pass-through exception is governed by the erratum on p. 6.
  • Setup bar count gives the number of units there during setup (user clarification). Heraldic track artwork has no additional rule identified.
  • The numbered reference map on p. 28 supplies locale labels for later tracing. Locale polygons, approach identities, exact narrow/wide classification, road junctions and every symbol occurrence still require map extraction.

Approach penalties

Infantry penalty

penalty-infantry

If defending pieces block this defense approach and the attack leading units are infantry, subtract one from the initial attack result. The matching penalty is applied once to the total, not once per leading unit. Infantry also has a separate minus-one modifier against a blocked defense approach, independent of these symbols. Ordinary defense from reserve does not trigger the symbol penalty; optional Santon rules are an exception. Artillery-led attacks still apply a matching artillery penalty but do not subtract defense leading-unit strength.

Preserve which side of the approach contains the symbol; do not interpret it as an extra movement cost.

Record with each instance: Approach ID + destination locale ID + position + rotation

Obstructed

penalty-obstructed

If the defense approach is obstructed, cavalry cannot lead an attack, lead a defense or counter-attack; cavalry may still participate without leading or conduct a feint. Separately, a declared Guard Attack is forbidden if the attacker’s own attack approach is obstructed. These two checks use opposite sides of the shared boundary.

Locale structure

Locale capacity

locale-capacity

Boxed number: maximum units of a player in the entire locale, excluding commanders; blocks, not strength. No separate approach/reserve capacity. A corps may pass through a locale by road even if the corps exceeds its capacity, provided the locale is not already full and the corps does not end there (erratum). During French setup, a corps may initially be assigned more units than its setup locale can hold only if the subsequent detachments leave capacity limits satisfied. Retreat destinations must obey capacity.

Record with each instance: Locale ID + position; preserve printed numeric value

Hill marker and elevation

hill

Triangle identifies a hill locale; the numeric elevation is cosmetic. Hill status matters: artillery-led attacks may occur from the same approach in consecutive rounds when the attack locale is a hill and the defense locale is not. Optional Santon rules restrict artillery other than the French fixed battery from leading attacks/defenses there. A fixed battery set up in Santon stays in reserve, may lead across any of its three approaches, and approach penalties apply even against its reserve defense.

Keep the hill flag separate from the printed elevation value and from background elevation shading.

Objective marker

objective-example

Star marking an objective locale. Blue, green, red and black form distinct victory categories; a locale can carry several categories. Objective control requires a corps with at least one infantry and/or artillery unit, plus a road path to a friendly main-road reinforcement entry locale; neither that entry nor any locale on the path may be enemy-occupied. Without French reinforcements, Allies win marginally if they control at least one blue objective and the French control no green, red or black objectives; otherwise the French win. With French reinforcements, French win marginally if they control at least one objective of each green, red and black category and the Allies control no blue objectives; otherwise Allies win. Demoralization takes precedence as a decisive victory.

Preserve every colour in a stacked group; do not collapse it to a single objective flag.

Record with each instance: Locale ID + position + printed colour/rotation; nearby label kept separately

entry-example

Reinforcement entry locale marker. Blue indicates French; red indicates Allied. Choose an unoccupied-by-enemy entry locale when bringing pieces on; entry uses road movement and commands. Roads

Main road

road-main

Main road: red for main roads, brown for local roads. A road move may travel up to three locales using only main roads, or up to two using local roads or a mixture. It must follow connected roads: routes in the same locale can be unconnected. From a starting locale with unconnected roads, a piece may leave by any one of them. Road moves normally start and end in reserve; cavalry may finish blocking an approach crossed by the connected route. Reinforcement entry requires road movement; objective control requires a road path to a friendly main-road entry.

Record with each instance: Route geometry + road class + connectivity within locales + crossed approaches; sharing a locale does not imply a junction.

Morale track

track-morale

Blue and red markers track French and Allied morale. The board marks initial levels as French 23 and Allied 27; optional balance rules may reduce initial morale. The losing army in an attack loses morale for its strength losses; retreat losses also reduce morale. The final demoralizing loss cannot come from an artillery-led attack. The first French reinforcement adds four morale. At night, recover half of morale losses, rounded down, to a maximum of four. Elite commitment and failed Guard Attacks have additional penalties. Demoralization ends the game immediately.
