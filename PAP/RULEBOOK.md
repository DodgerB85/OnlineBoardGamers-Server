# PERMITS & POISONS

## A game of bureaucratic alchemy and cutthroat commerce for 2–5 players

*Somberwick runs on two things: magic and paperwork. The magic gets the paperwork done; the paperwork gets the magic regulated. Somewhere in the gap, fortunes are made.*

You run a potion shop through seven fiscal Years. Build your workshop, brew to meet the city's needs, fund public Charters, and fight for customers on the High Street. **Permits** define where you may trade. **Poisons** define how far beyond good sense you are willing to go.

## The game at a glance

Each Year has four phases:

1. **Morning Post:** resolve the Council, events, market refresh, and other start-of-Year business.
2. **Morning Shift:** assign staff to gather ingredients, hire, upgrade, and craft.
3. **High Street:** sell potions through solo sales and contested Pricing Exchanges.
4. **Audit:** pay operating costs, inspect troubled shops, update turn order, and clean up.

After Year 7, the shop with the most Victory Points wins—provided it survives the Final Audit.

The central contest is the **Customer Score**:

```text
Customer Score = potion potency + Hype bid - committed price
```

The score decides **who wins** a contested sale. The winner's committed price decides **what they are paid**.

---

# 1. Setup

1. Each player takes an Emporium, staff pieces, **20 Crowns**, and a starting hand of **3 Paperwork cards**. Set Recognized Profit, Hype, Reputation, and Warnings to 0; set the Cauldron to Tier 1.
2. Choose a First Player by any method; “most recently audited” is traditional. Seating order is the Year 1 turn order.
3. Lay out the six fixed venues. Shuffle the Customer deck and deal one face down to each venue.
4. Each player chooses **2 House Special recipes**. A recipe may be chosen by no more than two players. These are your only starting recipes.
5. Check the opening market. Re-deal any customer whose Wants name a specific recipe that no player chose as a House Special in step 4. Customers with category, potency, or “anything” Wants always qualify.
6. Each player puts **1 Apprentice** into play on a Workshop tile of their choice. The other staff begin unemployed in the Ledger.
7. Separate and shuffle the main Paperwork deck and the Backed Futures/Expediting Fee side deck. Prepare the ingredient, recipe, Dispensation, Apparatus, event, and Council supplies.
8. Deal **3 Civic Charters** to the Council board.

## Market size

| Players | Customer spaces | Extra refresh rule |
|---:|---:|---|
| 2 | 6 | Refresh one additional customer each Morning Post: the venue whose customer has stood longest since its last refresh. |
| 3 | 6 | None. |
| 4 | 7 | Add 1 Overflow venue. |
| 5 | 8 | Add 2 Overflow venues. |

Each Overflow receives a fresh cheap customer from the Workhouse/Golem Pound cards every Morning Post and is accessible at Tier 1.

# 2. Tracks and currencies

## Cash and Recognized Profit

Each player's **Crown Ladder** has two tracks:

- **Cash** moves whenever Crowns enter or leave your shop.
- **Recognized Profit** moves only for operating income and operating outlay.

The visible gap between the two clips is your reinvested capital. Civic contributions use goods or Hype, never Crowns, and do not move either clip.

| Crown movement | Cash | Recognized Profit |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Sale income, dump income, Undercut Bonus, scale premium, unlicensed and Poison premiums | + | + |
| Serving overhead, Compliance Levies, fees, fines, bribes, Inspection Notices, Itinerant Franchise activation | − | − |
| Hires, upgrades, promotions, Access Tolls, and ingredient purchases | − | — |

Treat a Crown movement as **operating** only when it comes from selling, settling a sale, or surrendering to the Board. All growth and positioning costs are **capital**.

## Hype

Hype is capped at **20** and scores no VP.

Gain Hype when you:

- satisfy a customer: **+1**, or **+2** at a Panicked venue;
- complete a Board marketing initiative: **+2**, when directed by a card;
- contribute to a Civic Charter: **+1**;
- sell at a venue with a live Civic Boone: **+1 extra**;
- collect a live Founder's Cut.

Spend Hype to:

- print pamphlets for **2 Hype**, retargeting Panic or forcing a rival's Reliability check as directed;
- plant a Testimonial on your brand for **3 Hype**, creating a permanent demand magnet at one venue;
- fund a public demonstration for **4 Hype**, drawing a new customer into an empty venue;
- Expose a suspected over-tolerance sale for **1 Hype**;
- bid in a Pricing Exchange;
- pay any Hype cost printed on a card or Charter.

Locked Hype is unavailable until the effect that locked it ends.

## Reputation and Warnings

Reputation is tracked from **0–20**. Gain **+1 Reputation** for an Undercut Bonus. Lose **1 Reputation** when you dump a potion or take an uncancelled Warning.

Reputation is your final Pricing Exchange tie-break and your buffer against Condemnation. It scores no VP.

A Warning remains until cancelled by a Favor or another effect. Cancelling one restores the Reputation it cost; ignoring one does not remove it. A Tier 4 Cauldron may ignore one Warning each Audit.

# 3. The Morning Post

Resolve these steps in order:

1. **Public Review:** the eligible player may call a Public Review of the Stewardship.
2. **Council contributions:** players contribute to open Civic Charters in reverse Recognized-Profit order, lowest first. Resolve completed Charters immediately.
3. **Events:** reveal and resolve Scares and Testimonials. A Scare affects a fixed venue, not a customer type. A Testimonial attaches to a player's brand and remains there.
4. **Refresh customers:** reveal the opening customers in Year 1. In later Years, discard sold customers and customers left unserved last Year, then refill empty venues face up. Shuffle the discard pile if the deck runs out. A replacement for an unserved customer enters **Desperate**. Apply the player-count refresh rules from Setup.
5. **Paperwork:** each player draws **1** main-deck Paperwork card, respecting the hand limit.
6. **Start-of-Year choices:** eligible players may buy an Itinerant Franchise Charter; rivals may Expose previous-Phase Poison sales; resolve other Morning Post cards.
7. **Hype spending:** where order matters, resolve Hype spending in reverse turn order, last player first.

---

# 4. The Morning Shift

In forward turn order, assign available staff to your private Workshop. Rivals never block your tiles. You may assign any number of staff to a tile; each assigned piece takes that action once this Year, provided it meets the rank requirement.

Only the First Player uses **Street Alchemist** and **Cauldron Works** for free. Every other player pays a **1-Crown Access Toll per staff piece** assigned to either tile. Access Tolls are capital costs.

## Workshop actions

| Tile | Required rank | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Ingredient Co-op** | Any | Take 1 ingredient of your choice; Banshee costs 1 Crown. **Or** hire a staff member no higher than the acting piece's rank. |
| **Mushroom Crown** | Any | Take 2 Toadstool, or 1 Toadstool and 1 ingredient of your choice. |
| **Crystal Quarry** | Any | Take 2 Crystal, or 1 Crystal and 1 ingredient of your choice. |
| **The Kettle** | Journeyman or Master | Craft 1 potion. |
| **Street Alchemist** | Journeyman or Master | Draft 1 Dispensation, Apparatus, Market Recipe, or side-deck Paperwork card; **or** take any 2 ingredients. |
| **Cauldron Works** | Any to promote · Journeyman+ to upgrade to Tier 2/3 · Master to upgrade to Tier 4 or take 3 ingredients | Promote yourself or an idle staff member one rank if a slot is open; upgrade the Cauldron; **or** take any 3 ingredients. |

If a card changes an ingredient cost or craft requirement for the Year, apply it to every affected action.

## Staff

Hiring uses an Ingredient Co-op action. The acting staff member may recruit only its own rank or lower.

| Rank hired | Cost |
|---|---:|
| Apprentice | 1 Crown |
| Journeyman | 3 Crowns |
| Master | 6 Crowns |

New hires occupy a roster slot immediately but cannot act until the next Year's Morning Shift.

| Cauldron Tier | Maximum active roster |
|---:|---|
| 1 | 1 Apprentice, promotable in place to 1 Journeyman |
| 2 | 2 Apprentices, 1 Journeyman |
| 3 | 3 Apprentices, 2 Journeymen, 1 Master |
| 4 | 5 Apprentices, 3 Journeymen, 1 Master |

Any active staff member may spend a Cauldron Works action to promote itself or an idle staff member one rank. Promotion costs no Crowns but requires an open slot at the new rank. There is only one Master slot.

## Cauldron upgrades

A Journeyman or Master at Cauldron Works may upgrade the Cauldron **once per Year** (Tier 3 → 4 requires a Master).

| Upgrade | Rank required | Cost | Benefits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 → 2 | Journeyman or Master | 6 Crowns + 2 ingredients of any kind | Quicksilver counts double toward recipes; Kettle capacity 4; protect 1 potion from spoilage each Audit. |
| Tier 2 → 3 | Journeyman or Master | 12 Crowns + 1 Crystal + 1 Toadstool + 1 Quicksilver | Kettle capacity 5; 1 Apparatus slot. |
| Tier 3 → 4 | Master | 20 Crowns + 1 Banshee + 2 ingredients of any kind | Kettle capacity 6; second Apparatus slot; ignore 1 Warning each Audit. |

Tier 1 Kettle capacity is **3**. Capacity is the maximum number of potions you may hold in the Kettle.

## Crafting

Each Journeyman or Master assigned to The Kettle crafts one potion:

1. Choose a Base Recipe in your public recipe pool.
2. Spend its listed ingredients. Crafting has no Crown cost unless an effect says otherwise.
3. Optionally attach **one Dispensation** from your public arsenal.
4. Optionally add up to **3 Poison markers**, paying 1 ingredient of your choice per marker.
5. Put the potion in your Kettle. It may be sold this Year.

One craft action makes one potion. The Second Kettle Apparatus grants one additional craft each Year without staff.

At Audit, every unsold potion spoils unless protected:

- Tier 2+ protects one potion each Audit.
- A Cold-Room Rack protects one additional potion.
- Backed Futures protects its designated potion through one extra Audit while locking 2 Hype; return the Hype when that potion sells or spoils.
- Sabotage overrides all spoilage protection unless countered.

## Recipes, Dispensations, and Apparatus

- You permanently learn the Reward Recipe printed on each customer you satisfy.
- Street Alchemist may instead draft any Market Recipe directly into your public recipe pool.
- No other effect adds a recipe unless it explicitly says so.
- A drafted Dispensation is public, reusable, and returns to your arsenal after crafting. Attaching one is free unless its card says otherwise; a potion can never carry more than one.
- Apparatus are public and permanent once mounted. Tier 3 has one slot and Tier 4 has two; mounted Apparatus cannot be removed.
- A Danger-Twist's **Scare Scenario** never adds Crowns or a new score source. At a Panicked venue it grants the normal 2 Hype and uses the same Reliability check.

The cards contain their ingredient formulas and individual effects. Card text overrides the general rules.

# 5. The High Street

Resolve the High Street in two passes: **solo sales first**, then **contested Pricing Exchanges**. A player may make several sales in a Phase, but may enter no more than **one contested exchange**.

## Venues

You may offer at a venue only if your Cauldron meets its Tier requirement and your potion satisfies the current customer's Wants.

| Venue | Anchor | Required Tier | Serving overhead | Tolerated Poison | Founder's Cut |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Workhouse | 3 | 1 | 1 Crown | 2 | 1 Hype |
| Golem Pound | 5 | 1 | 1 Crown | 2 | 1 Hype |
| Lantern & Ledger | 6 | 2 | 1 Crown | 1 | 1 Hype |
| Fever Ward | 7 | 3 | 1 Crown | 1 | 2 Hype |
| Odditorium | 8 | 3 | 2 Crowns | 1 | 2 Hype |
| Nobles' Ring | 9 | 4 | 3 Crowns | 0 | 2 Hype |

Overflow venues are Tier 1. Every realized venue sale pays its listed serving overhead as an operating cost. Dumps pay no serving overhead.

The **Anchor** is the highest price that customer will pay. It never increases because of mood, Reputation, a Civic Boone, Poison, or any other non-card effect.

## Customers and moods

A Customer card shows its Wants, mood, Preference, Anchor, and Reward Recipe. Any potion meeting the Wants can satisfy it.

| Mood | Payment | Additional effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Calm** | Normal | None. |
| **Desperate** | Normal first sale; each additional qualifying potion sold to that same satisfied customer pays the Anchor. | The player's total Desperate-volume sales this Phase cannot exceed their Kettle capacity. These extra sales are solo volume and do not count as additional Pricing Exchanges. The Reward Recipe is learned only once. |
| **Panicked** | Normal | Gain 2 Hype instead of the usual 1 and make a Reliability check. |

### Reliability checks

When you sell at a Panicked venue, make an over-tolerance sale, or another effect calls for a Reliability check, compare the delivered potion with the customer's printed Preference:

- If the customer has no Preference, or the potion has the matching Preference tag, the check passes.
- Otherwise the sale still completes and is paid, but you take **1 Warning** and lose **1 Reputation**.

At a Calm venue, a clean sale's unmet Preference only loses the Preference tie-break — it never triggers a Reliability check. Panic never closes a venue or cancels a fitted Dispensation. Card effects such as Properly Licensed Doom may override this check.

An over-tolerance sale always forfeits the normal Preference safe harbor, so it takes the Reliability Warning even if the Preference would otherwise match, unless a specific card overrides the check. A successful Expose can add a second Warning later.

## Solo sales

If only one player can currently satisfy a customer, that player may sell at exactly the customer's Anchor. No bid occurs.

Eligibility, not willingness, decides this: if two or more players could satisfy a customer, resolve it as a Pricing Exchange even if only one of them chooses to sell. A lone Exchange commitment still names its own price — it is not automatically paid the Anchor — and cannot earn the Undercut Bonus, since there is no rival price to undercut.

Selling is optional. If a shop could serve a customer but declines and the customer remains unserved, that shop loses **1 Reputation**. If several shops could serve it, each shop that declined to bid suffers the loss. The customer expires and is replaced as Desperate next Morning Post.

## Pricing Exchanges

If at least two players can satisfy the same customer, each participating player secretly commits:

- one qualifying potion;
- a whole-number price from **1 Crown through the customer's Anchor**;
- any amount of Hype to spend.

Reveal together and calculate:

```text
Customer Score = potion potency + Hype bid - committed price
```

Highest score wins. Break ties in this order:

1. matching Preference;
2. lower committed price;
3. earlier position in the venue's current Phase C order;
4. higher Reputation.

The winner receives **their own committed price**, not the Anchor, then pays the venue's serving overhead. All committed Hype is spent.

If the winner's price was strictly lower than every rival's, they also gain the **Undercut Bonus: +2 Crowns and +1 Reputation**. Tying the lowest price earns no bonus. Winning above the lowest price has no penalty and no bonus.

### Re-offers

A potion that loses an exchange may be offered once more to a **different** customer or venue that is still available this Phase. Resolve all re-offers after all first-pass exchanges, in the venues' fixed Phase C order. A potion may be re-offered only once per Phase, and a completed sale is final.

A re-offer is a retry, not a source of extra volume. If it becomes a sale, it still uses an available customer and cannot break the venue cap.

## Extra volume and the venue cap

Each venue can serve at most **2 customers per Phase**, using no more than **one extra-customer source**.

- A Civic Boone may add one customer, raising the venue to 2 but never 3.
- An Itinerant Franchise second till may add one customer, raising the venue to 2 but never 3.
- If both apply, the Boone yields; they do not stack.
- Desperate volume adds potions to one already-satisfied customer rather than another customer. It is bounded by Kettle capacity and does not combine with another extra-volume benefit at that venue.
- Re-offers add no customers of their own.

## Dumping

You may dump an unsold potion for **1 Crown** without an exchange. A dump has no serving overhead, earns no Itinerant Franchise scale premium, and costs **1 Reputation**. It is operating income.

## Itinerant Franchise Charter

At the Morning Post, a player at least **10 Recognized Profit behind the current leader** may pay **2 Crowns** to charter their shop for the current Year. The cost is operating outlay.

For the rest of that Year:

- the shop may serve one additional customer at any venue it already qualifies for, subject to the venue cap;
- every real solo sale or Pricing Exchange win earns a **+8-Crown scale premium** as operating income;
- the second till resolves as solo volume and does not permit a second contested exchange;
- dumps receive no premium.

The Charter lasts for the entire Year even if the shop closes the gap. It must be bought again next Year, and only if the shop is still at least 10 behind. All normal fees, Warnings, capacity, spoilage, Inspections, and Audit rules still apply.

# 6. The Audit

Resolve the Audit in this order:

## 1. Settle fees and Compliance Levies

Clean and within-tolerance sales owe no Compliance Levy. Each over-tolerance sale owes exactly **2 Crowns**. Pay Levies as one aggregate amount without identifying the potion or venue that caused them. A card may double them.

Compliance Levies are operating costs. They are not Access Tolls, which are capital costs.

## 2. Resolve Warnings and Condemnation

Apply effects that cancel or ignore Warnings. If your remaining Warnings exceed your Reputation, you are **Condemned**. Either:

- pay **2 Crowns per excess Warning**; or
- discard one Journeyman to cancel the excess.

## 3. Make the Ruin Check

Use the Audit Watch assigned at the previous Audit. Its player is **Under Administration** next Year only if both are true:

1. they are on the Audit Watch and at or below the 40-Crown Contribution Threshold; and
2. their cumulative Recognized Profit did not grow this Year.

Administration is a tax, not a shutdown. Next Year, that player:

- acts, hires, promotes, crafts, and sells normally;
- cannot claim First Player;
- names one rival, who then takes one ingredient of their choice from the administered player's store, until the end of that Year;
- gains **1 Warning** and loses **1 Reputation**;
- may be targeted by a rival paying **1 Crown** for a **+1 Customer Score edge** in one Pricing Exchange against them.

Administration ends when the player is no longer the watched, non-improving shop.

## 4. Recalibrate turn order

The player with the highest **net Recognized Profit earned this Year** takes First Player for next Year and gains **1 Warning**, losing 1 Reputation unless that Warning is cancelled.

Break ties by fewest Warnings, then by whoever reached the tied amount first. A player Under Administration cannot claim First Player.

## 5. Assign the Audit Watch

Place the Watch beside the player with the lowest cumulative Recognized Profit who is at or below the **40-Crown Contribution Threshold**. If everyone is above 40, watch the player with the lowest Recognized Profit.

The Watch alone causes no fee, Warning, or seizure. It only identifies the shop eligible for next Year's Ruin Check and the one closest to the Final Audit.

## 6. Clean up

- Remove each Civic Boone that has reached its Audit; its extra Hype, extra volume, and live Founder's Cut end. The Founder's Seal remains.
- Discard all unprotected unsold potions. Resolve protection durations and return Hype when Backed Futures ends.
- Return staff from Workshop tiles to their available areas. Newly hired staff become available at the next Morning Shift.
- Advance the Year marker. After Year 7, proceed to scoring; otherwise begin the next Morning Post.

# 7. The Permit Office

The Council keeps **2–3 open Civic Charters**. Each names a target good—such as a recipe class, ingredient donation, or Crown pledge—and a shared completion threshold, usually **3–5 contributions**.

The first Charters are dealt during Setup. Founders choose later Charter targets through Bylaw votes.

## Contributions

During the Morning Post, resolve contributions in reverse cumulative Recognized-Profit order, lowest first.

- Each player may contribute to each open Charter **once per Charter per Year**.
- Give one unit of the named good from your private stores to advance the shared clip by one. It never returns.
- A Crown pledge is civic credit paid in Hype, never physical Crowns.
- Gain **1 Hype** for each contribution.
- Contributions from all players use one shared clip. A spent contribution slot remains spent even if the Charter does not pass.

## Passing a Charter: Civic Boone

When the shared clip reaches its threshold, the Charter passes and its venue gains a **Civic Boone** until the next Audit:

- every sale there grants **+1 additional Hype**;
- the venue may serve one additional customer, subject to the 2-customer cap and non-stacking volume rules;
- the Boone never changes price, Anchor, serving overhead, Poison tolerance, or any Crown amount.

Everyone may use the Boone, including the Founder and the Recognized-Profit leader.

After resolving the Boone and any Founder's Seal, use a Bylaw vote to open a replacement Charter so the Council returns to 2–3 open Charters.

## Founder's Seal

The eligible contributor whose contribution completes the Charter claims that venue's Founder's Seal. If simultaneous contributions would complete it, the lower-Recognized eligible contributor resolves first. The current Recognized-Profit leader can contribute and gain Hype but can never claim a Seal or Founder's Cut. If the leader is the only contributor, the Charter passes without a Founder.

A Founder's Seal is permanent and has three effects:

1. **Exclusivity while the Boone is live:** the venue cannot receive another Charter or Founder, though all players may still sell there.
2. **Founder's Cut while the Boone is live:** gain the venue's printed **+1 or +2 Hype per sale** cut. It is never Crowns and never changes price.
3. **Permanent Bylaw vote:** each Seal grants one vote when choosing the next Charter target.

When the Boone fades, exclusivity and the Founder's Cut end. The Seal and its vote remain, and the venue may later be chartered again.

## Bylaw votes

When a new Charter target is needed, each player has one vote per Founder's Seal held. The target with the most votes becomes the next Charter. Break a tie in favor of the tied voter with the lowest Recognized Profit.

Contributors without a Seal do not vote. If the chosen target is a Crown pledge, contributions to it are paid in Hype.

## Public Review of the Stewardship

Once per Year, before Council contributions:

- With 4–5 players, the player second in Recognized Profit may call a Review without consent.
- With 3 players, the lowest-Recognized non-leader may call it.
- With 2 players, Public Review is disabled.

Calling a Review consumes the caller's next contribution slot that Year **before the current Charter's outcome is known**.

For one Charter opportunity, the Recognized-Profit leader is frozen out of the civic lane: they cannot contribute, Found a Charter, or collect a Founder's Cut. Production, sales, unit economics, and the leader's share of a Boone remain unchanged.

Each rival with a Founder's Seal whose Boone is still live may Rally, extending the freeze by one additional Charter opportunity.

When the Review ends, the leader's barred contribution to the next open Charter is made by the Review instead and counts **double** toward its threshold. It is recorded in the leader's name but awards no Hype or private control of the Charter. No Crowns or goods are confiscated from the leader.

# 8. Poisons and toxicity

When crafting, you may add up to **3 face-down Poison markers** to a potion. Each marker:

- costs 1 ingredient of your choice;
- adds **+1 potency**;
- raises the potion's Poison level by 1.

The markers and their count remain hidden. The venue tolerances are public.

## Legal and over-tolerance sales

If the potion's Poison level is at or below the venue's tolerance, the sale is legal. The potion keeps its potency bonus and cannot be successfully Exposed.

If its Poison level is above tolerance, the sale is **over-tolerance** and treated as unlicensed-strength:

- gain a **+2-Crown unlicensed premium**;
- gain a **+3-Crown Poison premium**;
- owe a **2-Crown Compliance Levy** at Audit;
- remain vulnerable to Reliability consequences and Expose.

The premiums come from the black market, not the customer. They do not change the committed price or breach the Anchor. After the Levy, an over-tolerance sale earns exactly **3 Crowns more than the same clean sale**:

```text
+2 unlicensed premium + 3 Poison premium - 2 Compliance Levy = +3 Crowns
```

Premiums are operating income; the Levy is operating outlay. Poison ingredients are capital costs.

## Expose

At the next Morning Post, a rival may pay **1 Hype** and name a venue where you sold during the previous High Street.

- If you made any over-tolerance sale there, take **1 Warning** and lose 1 Reputation.
- If every sale there was clean or within tolerance, nothing happens and the Hype is lost.

Levies are paid as an unnamed aggregate, so rivals know that some violation occurred but not which venue caused it. The seller keeps the marker record private until the Expose window closes.

# 9. Cards and timing

Individual recipes, customers, Dispensations, Apparatus, Scares, and Testimonials are fully described on their cards. Resolve the card's timing, cost, and effect as printed; specific card text overrides this rulebook.

## Scares and Testimonials

- A Scare changes the named fixed venue or applies its printed Year-long condition. Panicked venues pay normally, grant 2 Hype, and require Reliability checks.
- A Testimonial attaches to a player's brand permanently. Resolve venue-specific or ongoing effects from the card.

## Paperwork

Start with 3 main-deck cards and draw 1 each Year. The hand limit is **4**, or **5** with the Paperweight Plinth. Side-deck cards enter play only through Street Alchemist.

| Paper | Timing and cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Favor** | Audit; free | Cancel one Warning or make a chosen rival resolve the Audit's fee check before you. It may instead name a saboteur for restitution: if their later Sabotage lands on your potion that Year, they pay 2 Crowns or take a Warning. |
| **Bribe** | Morning Post or High Street; 2 Crowns | Retarget a Scare to a rival's venue, or count your Hype bid as +2 in a closed Pricing Exchange. |
| **Sabotage Order** | High Street; 2 Hype | Spoil one rival potion immediately, overriding Backed Futures and Protect-1. |
| **Inspection Notice** | Any time; 1 Crown | A rival immediately pays all outstanding Compliance Levies or takes a Warning. This does not trigger Condemnation. |
| **Expediting Fee** | Audit; 1 Crown | Skip one of your fees or make a rival resolve last this Phase. |
| **Backed Futures** | Morning Post; lock 2 Hype | Protect one named potion through one extra Audit. Return the Hype when it sells or spoils. |

### Countering Sabotage

The target of a Sabotage Order may use one applicable response:

- **Fire-Ward:** spend 2 Reputation to make one potion immune to Sabotage for the Phase.
- **Favor restitution:** play a Favor naming the culprit; if a later Sabotage lands on your potion that Year, the culprit pays 2 Crowns or takes a Warning.
- **Whistling Cork:** redirect the destroyed potion's Sabotage to the saboteur's Kettle, as described on the Dispensation.

# 10. Endgame and victory

After Year 7's Audit, calculate VP. Divide and round down where a rate applies.

| Source | VP |
|---|---:|
| Recognized Profit | 1 VP per 3 Crowns |
| Cauldron Tier 2 / 3 / 4 | 3 / 7 / 11 VP |
| Worked staff roster | +3 VP if every staff piece you ever employed was assigned to a tile at least once |
| Unspent Cash | 1 VP per 8 Crowns |
| Uncancelled Warnings | −2 VP each |

A Cauldron Tier scores only if reached by the **start of Year 7**. A Year 7 upgrade scores no Tier VP. A staff member who joined your roster in Year 7 cannot satisfy the worked-roster condition, since it cannot act that Year.

Hype and Reputation score no VP.

## Final Audit

The **Contribution Threshold is 40 cumulative Recognized Profit**, visible from Year 1.

- A shop below 40 is Audited Into Ruin and cannot win, regardless of its other VP.
- A shop at 40 or above remains eligible.
- If even the Recognized-Profit leader is below 40, nobody is eliminated.

Optional softer ending: a player below 40 may accept a permanent **−10 VP** instead of elimination and remain in the ranking.

Among eligible shops, the highest total VP wins.

# 11. Rules precedence and unresolved disputes

- Specific card text overrides a general rule.
- If no specific rule resolves an ambiguity, default to charging 1 Crown or consuming one staff action, whichever fits the action.
- If a dispute still cannot be resolved, the player with fewer Crowns wins the ruling. The city considers them more plausibly oppressed.

*The Fiscal Year is up. Total your Recognized Profit and be glad the clerk was too busy to notice what you got away with.*
