Last updated: 9 November 2026

OnlineBoardGamers ("we", "us") runs this website. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your username, email address, password (stored as a hash, never in plain text), language preference and notification settings.
  • Sign-in data: if you sign in with Google or Discord, we receive an account identifier and your email address from that provider. We never receive your Google or Discord password.
  • Discord notifications: if you link Discord for direct messages, we store your Discord user ID and add you to the OnlineBoardGamers Discord server.
  • Gameplay data: games you create or join, moves, chat messages, tournament results, statistics and related timestamps.
  • Technical data: session, CSRF and language cookies, and basic server logs used to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

  • To run your account, games, tournaments and notifications.
  • To send the notification emails you have enabled, such as turn, invite and reminder emails. You can change these in your profile.
  • To investigate abuse, bugs and security issues.

Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We use service providers for hosting, database, email delivery and, where you enable them, Discord, Slack or Telegram notifications.
  • We may disclose data where required by law.

Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in, a CSRF cookie to protect forms, and a language cookie to remember your language. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and gameplay data while your account exists, because it is part of the games and tournaments you take part in. You can request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting us.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: admin@onlineboardgamers.com.
