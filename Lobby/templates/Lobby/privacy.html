Last updated: 9 November 2026

Online Board Gamers ("Online Board Gamers", "we", "us") operates https://www.onlineboardgamers.com/ (the "Service"). This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your username, email address, password (stored as a hash, never in plain text), language preference, notification settings and other profile preferences.

Google account information

If you choose "Sign in with Google", we use Google OAuth to authenticate you. We request the openid, email and profile scopes. We receive and store your Google account identifier (the sub value) and your email address. We do not receive or store your Google password, contacts, or profile picture. If you create an account with Google, we use that email address to create your account and mark it as verified.

Discord account information

If you sign in with Discord or link Discord for direct-message notifications, we receive and store your Discord user ID and, for sign-in, your email address. We never receive your Discord password.

Gameplay information

We store the games you create or join, moves, chat messages, tournament results, statistics and related timestamps. This data is needed to run games and is visible to the other players in those games.

Technical information

We keep basic server logs (including IP address and request details) for security, abuse prevention and debugging. We also use session, CSRF and language cookies.

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-XVXK8Q50KN) to understand aggregate traffic and usage. Google Analytics may set cookies and collect information such as your IP address, device and browser details, pages visited and approximate location. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising.

How we use information

  • To create and operate your account and authenticate you.
  • To run games, tournaments, notifications, statistics and replays.
  • To send the notification emails you have enabled. You can change these in your profile.
  • To investigate abuse, fraud, bugs and security issues.
  • To understand aggregate usage and improve the Service.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We use Google user data only to authenticate you and provide the Service. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, use it to train AI models, or use it to create or distribute AI-generated imagery. We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide the Service, to comply with law, or with your consent.

Sharing

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We use service providers for hosting, database, email delivery, analytics and, where you enable them, Discord, Slack or Telegram notifications.
  • We may disclose data where required by law or to protect the Service and its users.

Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in, a CSRF cookie to protect forms, a language cookie to remember your language, and Google Analytics cookies to measure aggregate usage. We do not use advertising cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser, but blocking the session cookie will prevent you from logging in.

Security

We use HTTPS, hash passwords, and limit access to personal data to people who need it to operate the Service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

We keep account data while your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data, except where we must keep records for legal or security reasons, or where gameplay records are needed for other players' games and tournament history. Server logs are kept for a limited period.

Your choices and rights

  • You can change your email notification settings in your profile.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • You can revoke our access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access does not delete your Online Board Gamers account; contact us if you also want the account and its Google link removed.
  • You can stop Discord direct messages from your profile.
  • If you are in a region with data protection law, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date shown above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: admin@onlineboardgamers.com.
