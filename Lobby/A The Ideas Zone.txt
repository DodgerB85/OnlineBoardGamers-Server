* Details of our tournament: players sign up using google forms. They must have played at least 4 games of TGZ on boardgamecore. 
For Round 1 put them into groups of 7 where they play 4 games each, meeting each other player in the group twice. 
Finalists from the last tournament and winners of previous tournaments are distributed among the groups, the remaining 6 spaces in each group are random. 
The number of groups depends on the number of signups. Based on wins (with tiebreakers) we take 14 people from Round 1 into two groups of 7 for Round 2. 
The top 2 from each Round 2 group then go into a Final game. So Round 1 varies, but so far Round 2 and the final remain the same.

We likely want the ability to change group size, number of games played, whether they meet each other twice or once or at most twice etc. 
This is something vraid has been doing the math on. If we do have extra players remaining after making the groups, 
they don't get to be in the tournament and are kept as replacements. First come first served. 
I have players that are banned for the next tournament for taking too long and being a dick about it when reminded, 
so they would be manually removed if they return.

I think we want to avoid a hard kick-out. People often have good reasons for the occasional delay.
It's just repeat offenders that slow the show. We would need the ability to replace players and restart 
those games without it messing up whatever is tracking the tournament. In siddhig's site we just update the game links.

Initially we can run the tournament separately as before, then look into integrating depending on how much enthusiasm you have at that point. 
Maybe a good first step would be for us would be to have siddhigs functionality integrated into the site, so everyone can see the status of the tournament.

The automated tournaments you do would be cool too, they can co-exist.

Oh and while I think of it, both outside and inside of tournaments, being able to have open invites for games, 
but with the requirement that players have played at least N games, or have some kind of status like veteran, 
beginner etc would be a nice feature for players looking for certain levels.
