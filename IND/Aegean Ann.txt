What is this?
=============
An unofficial fan-made amp, based around the Aegean.
The trusty Indonesian gameplay with a spicy Mediterranean twist!

How does it play?
=================
The rules are exactly the same as Indonesia - the changes come in the map!
With a deliberate grouping of goods in distinct areas, longer shipping lines are generally required. 
This map focuses more on the logistics of shipping your goods over larger distances. 
Games will generally last slightly longer than the Indonesian map.

Has it been play-tested?
=======================
Yes! Changes have been made to give it more balance. 
Algorithms were used to help create the map, in order to achieve a good balance within the game.  
Minor tweaks can still be made if more feedback comes with more plays on this map. 

Given the different game style between this and the original map, Aegean games will be excluded from the stats
