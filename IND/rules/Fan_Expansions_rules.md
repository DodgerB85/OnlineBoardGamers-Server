## Aegean

![Aegean map](/static/IND/images/maps/AG_map.jpg)

Aegean is a fan-made alternate map. It uses the normal Indonesia rules; there are no additional rules or optional modules. Select "Use Aegean Map" when creating a game.

## Pilipinas (PHP)

![Pilipinas map](/static/IND/images/maps/PH_map.jpg)

Pilipinas is a fan-made expansion for Indonesia. Splotter Spellen was not involved in design or playtesting, and thus this expansion should not be viewed as a canonical addition to the base game.

While Indonesia's base game has fantastic depth, we've always wished that we could continue exploring it with new maps and mechanics. Pilipinas brings the experience of Indonesia to the archipelago of the Philippines with variable map setup and a larger toolkit for players to use. This expansion borrows the Philippines as a setting for players to tell their own story. It does not adhere to the true geography or history of the archipelago.

### Components

A copy of Indonesia is required to play Pilipinas.

- 1 game board
- 10 player tokens (2 in each color)
- 24 company cards (15 double-sided)

### Setup

Follow setup as normal with the following changes:

1. **Map:** Place the board with the Pilipinas map face up in the center of the play area.
2. **Company cards:** Separate Pilipinas company cards by era. Randomly remove 2 production companies of different types from Era A, and 2 production companies of different types from Era B (4 removed companies in total; e.g. 1 rice and 1 spice from Era A, 1 spice and 1 rubber from Era B). Flip the 4 removed companies to the side showing oil and add them to Era C. Lay out the remaining Era A companies on the board.
3. **R&D:** Place tokens from each player on the first position of the used R&D upgrade tracks.
4. **City cards:** Separate Pilipinas city cards by era (a, b, c), then deal one card from each era to each player.

### Rules changes on the Pilipinas map

**One-way arrows:** There are now one-way arrows that connect islands. Companies may only expand in the direction of the arrow.

**Siap Saji companies:** Previously, Siap Saji could only be formed when merging Rice and Spice together. There is now a company card that starts as Siap Saji and may be merged with other Siap Saji companies.

**Ship redeployment:** Immediately after 2 shipping companies merge, the winner of the auction may return all but 1 ship from each sea zone the company occupies.

### Optional R&D modules

Additional upgrades are available to research in the R&D phase. Players should decide which (if any) of these modules to use prior to starting the game. Both are selected as game options when creating a Pilipinas game.

#### Merger Subsidy

Merger Subsidy indicates the amount a player's maximum bid is increased as well as the maximum the bank will pay opponents after winning a merger. Each step on the Merger Subsidy R&D track is worth 100 rupiah. The Merger Subsidy is only used to purchase goods from opponents and never used to pay for a player's own goods.

> **Example 1:** Player A has a size 2 rice company. Player B has a size 3 rice company. Player A has a Merger Subsidy of 100 and wins the merger with a bid of 150. The bank pays Player B 90.

> **Example 2:** Player A has a size 3 spice company and a size 1 spice company. Player A has a Merger Subsidy of 100 and wins the merger with a bid of 200. No money is exchanged.

#### Shipping Subsidy

Shipping Subsidy indicates the amount a player will receive from the bank for each good an opponent delivers using their shipping line. Each step on the Shipping Subsidy R&D track is worth 5 rupiah per good. A player does not earn the subsidy when using their own shipping line.
