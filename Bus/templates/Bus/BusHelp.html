{% translate "Playing the Game" %}
    {% blocktranslate trimmed %}
  • Check your connection to the live update server by looking at the circle next to your name in the upper right.
    Orange = Connecting, Green = Connected, Red = Disconncedted
  • The active player has a green background in the Players table
  • In addition, after actions have been chosen the current move is highlighted in green in the action selection area
  • During the "Choose Actions" phase, any player that has passed will have an orange background
  • Place buildings, lines, and passengers directly on the board. Vrrooomm by selecting passengers and destinations on the board
  • Choose your actions in the upper right area or next to the board
  • The Players table is in order of the "Choose Actions" phase. So top player picks first
    If no one chooses the "Starting Player" choice, then the player on the 2nd line will become the new start player.
  • To view another player's actions, open the "History" tab and select a "board" action to highlight that move
  • Score is calculated as you play - Time Stone deductions happen immediately, not at the end
  • Any player can rewind at any time. Please be courteous. Misuse will result in alteration of this feature
  • You can change which board you are playing on from the menu. Select a preference in your Profile
    NOTE: THE CHANGE IS COSMETIC ONLY. BOTH BOARDS HAVE THE SAME CONNECTIONS
    THE ORIGINAL BOARD IS ROTATED 90 DEGREES FROM THE 20TH ANNIVERSARY BOARD
    • {% endblocktranslate %}
{% translate "How can I find other players' moves?" %}
{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Click on History from the top menu to open the history, and then click on an entry to highlight where it happened on the board. You can click entries to see where buildings or lines were added, and to see where passengers were VRROOOMM'd from and to. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Why did I miss my turn?" %}
{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Turns where you cannot do anything are skipped. For example, during VRROOOMM if you either have no possible destination buildings, or no available passengers to move. During Line Expansion, Add Passengers and New Buildings then players are skipped if they would have 0 actions. In this case, it means that the Max number of Buses is too low for these players to have any actions. Check the history for the reason a player's turn was skipped. {% endblocktranslate %}
